01 · ABOUT

THE SEQUEL.

Hack-A-Thon 1.0 ran for 32 hours at SPEC. 2.0 runs longer, with more domains.

HACK-A-THON 2.0 is a 36-hour, international-level hackathon organised by the Department of CSE, St. Peter's Engineering College, with ORIGIN, the CSE & IT department association. Build a working prototype for a real problem in one of nine domains, with mentor reviews at four checkpoints along the way.

    09 · FAQ

    QUESTIONS.

    Who can participate?

    Any B.Tech undergraduate.

    How big can my team be?

    Between 3 and 5 members.

    How much does it cost?

    ₹599 per member, paid only after your abstract is selected (₹1,797 for a team of 3, up to ₹2,995 for 5). You pay by scanning a UPI QR on your Team Dashboard.

    When are problem statements released?

    On Day 1, right after the inauguration. See the timeline for the exact slot.

    How is judging done?

    Through four checkpoints. The first three are progress reviews, and Checkpoint 4 is the final judging round after code freeze.

    Is food provided?

    Meals and refreshments are scheduled through both days, including a night break. See the timeline.

    What should I bring?

    Laptops, chargers, your college ID and anything else you need to build.

    Can rules change?

    Rules and guidelines are subject to change. Decisions by organisers and judges are final.
