ABOUT

The Department Association Committee for the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and Information Technology (IT) departments at St. Peter’s Engineering College serves as a vibrant and dynamic platform dedicated to fostering innovation, collaboration, leadership, and technical excellence among students.

01 · Environment

The committee aims to create an engaging academic environment where students can explore emerging technologies, exchange ideas, develop practical skills, and transform their creative ideas into meaningful solutions.

02 · Activities

Through the organization of technical workshops, seminars, hackathons, coding competitions, project exhibitions, expert talks, industrial visits, and various academic and extracurricular activities, the committee encourages students to go beyond classroom learning and gain valuable real-world experience. It provides opportunities for students to interact with industry professionals, understand current technological trends, explore career opportunities, and bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry expectations.

03 · Skills

The committee also emphasizes the development of essential professional skills, including teamwork, communication, problem-solving, critical thinking, project management, and entrepreneurship. By encouraging active participation, peer learning, and collaborative initiatives, it empowers students to take on leadership roles, showcase their talents, and contribute to the growth of the department and the institution.

04 · Vision

Committed to academic excellence and continuous learning, the Department Association Committee strives to build a strong technical community that nurtures creativity, promotes research and innovation, and prepares students to meet the challenges of an evolving technological landscape. Through its initiatives, the committee aspires to inspire the next generation of engineers, innovators, and technology leaders while strengthening the connection between students, faculty, industry, and the broader professional community.
